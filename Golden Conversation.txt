1. Start the default five-period session
User: Alexa, open Vision Protect.
Alexa: Start five 20-minute focus periods?
User: Yes.
Alexa: Your first eye break is in 20 minutes.
Alexa then announces:
- 20:00: Eye break. Look away from the screen at something at least 20 feet away. Keep looking away for 30 seconds.
- 20:30: Your eye break is complete. Your focus period is resuming now.
- 40:30: Eye-break announcement.
- 41:00: Focus resumes.
- 61:00: Eye-break announcement.
- 61:30: Focus resumes.
- 81:30: Eye-break announcement.
- 82:00: Focus resumes.
- 102:00: Vision Protect is complete. Your 5 focus periods have ended. Consider taking a longer break before returning to the screen.
2. Choose the number of periods
User: Alexa, open Vision Protect.
Alexa: Start five 20-minute focus periods?
User: No.
Alexa: How many 20-minute focus periods would you like, from one to five?
User: Three.
Alexa: Your first eye break is in 20 minutes.
The three-period session ends after 61 minutes.
3. Start one period
User: Alexa, open Vision Protect.
Alexa: Start five 20-minute focus periods?
User: No.
Alexa: How many 20-minute focus periods would you like, from one to five?
User: One.
Alexa: Vision Protect started for one 20-minute focus period.
After 20 minutes:
Alexa: Vision Protect is complete. Your 1 focus period has ended. Consider taking a longer break before returning to the screen.
A one-period session has no eye-break reminder.
4. Start directly
User: Alexa, ask Vision Protect to start.
Alexa: Your first eye break is in 20 minutes.
Other supported phrases include:
- “Alexa, ask Vision Protect to begin.”
- “Alexa, ask Vision Protect to begin a session.”
- “Alexa, ask Vision Protect to start my screen break timer.”
- “Alexa, tell Vision Protect to start three periods.”
Starting another session replaces the existing Vision Protect session.
5. Request help
User: Alexa, ask Vision Protect how this works.
Alexa: Vision Protect schedules one to five 20-minute focus periods, with a 30 second eye break between periods. Five periods is the default. Say start Vision Protect to use the default, or say a number from one to five.
If the user is silent:
Alexa: Say start Vision Protect, or choose a number from one to five.
6. Enter an invalid number
User: Alexa, tell Vision Protect to start six periods.
Alexa: Please choose between one and five 20-minute focus periods.
If the user is silent:
Alexa: How many focus periods would you like, from one to five?
User: Four.
Alexa: Your first eye break is in 20 minutes.
7. Cancel an active session
User: Alexa, ask Vision Protect to cancel my session.
Alexa: Your Vision Protect session has been canceled.
All reminders belonging to the session are removed.
8. Cancel when nothing is active
User: Alexa, ask Vision Protect to cancel my session.
Alexa: There is no active Vision Protect session to cancel.
9. Reminders permission is missing
User: Alexa, open Vision Protect.
Alexa: Start five 20-minute focus periods?
User: Yes.
Alexa: Vision Protect needs permission to announce eye breaks during your screen-use session. Please enable Reminders permission in the Alexa app, then open Vision Protect again.
Alexa also displays a permission-consent card in the Alexa app.
10. Alexa does not understand
User: Alexa, ask Vision Protect to make coffee.
Alexa: I did not understand that. Say start Vision Protect, cancel Vision Protect, or help.
If the user is silent:
Alexa: What would you like to do?
11. Temporary service failure
If the session cannot start:
Alexa: I could not start the Vision Protect schedule right now. Please try again in a moment.
If cancellation fails:
Alexa: I could not cancel the Vision Protect reminders. Please try again.
12. Google Home
Google Home runs a fixed five-period automation.
User: Hey Google, start Vision Protect.
Google: Vision Protect has started. Look 20 feet away when you hear each eye break. Five focus periods end in 102 minutes.
Google announces four 30-second breaks during the session. At 102 minutes:
Google: Vision Protect is complete. Your five focus periods have ended. Consider taking a longer break before returning to the screen.
